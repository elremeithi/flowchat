## What's new in 1.8.3

- **Tap a QR code's title to copy its text.** In the QR codes section, tapping the title or the text under it copies the code's text and briefly shows *Copied*. Tapping the picture still shows the code full screen. Codes saved as a picture have no text, so their title shows them full screen.

## Upgrading

- **Server (Unraid)**: update the Flowchat container (use *force update* in Advanced View if Unraid says there's no update). Settings should then show *Server version 1.8.3*.
- **Android**: install `Flowchat-1.8.3.apk` over the current app (optional: the change comes from the server).
