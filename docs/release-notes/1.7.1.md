## What's new in 1.7.1

- **QR codes tab works in the Android app with any server version.** Pages from servers older than 1.7 sent the tab to the app's own QR codes screen and switched to Chat. The 1.7.1 app no longer answers that old hand-off, so the tab shows your codes in the page even before the server is updated. Without a connection, the app's own encrypted copy of your codes still opens, as in 1.7.0.

## Upgrading

- **Android**: install `Flowchat-1.7.1.apk` over the current app, then close and reopen it. This alone fixes the QR codes tab.
- **Server (Unraid)**: update the Flowchat container. In the page's Settings, the line under the device list should then show *Server version 1.7.1*. If there's no version line at all, the server is older than 1.6.
