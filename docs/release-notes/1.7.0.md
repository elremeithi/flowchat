## What's new in 1.7.0

- **QR codes tab works in the Android app.** With a connection to your server, the tab shows your codes right in the page, the same as in a browser; showing a code still turns the screen to full brightness. Without a connection, the app's own encrypted copy of your codes opens instead.
- **The app keeps its offline copy of your codes current**, syncing whenever the app opens or goes to the background.
- **If the app's QR codes screen can't open**, it now says why instead of closing silently, and offers a report you can copy.
- **Settings** shows the server and app versions.

## Upgrading

- **Server (Unraid)**: update the Flowchat container. Settings should then show *Server version 1.7*.
- **Android**: install `Flowchat-1.7.0.apk` over the current app, then close and reopen it.
