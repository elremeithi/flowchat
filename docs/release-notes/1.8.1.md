## What's new in 1.8.1

- **Swipe between sections on phones.** In the Android app and in phone browsers, swipe left or right to move between Chat, Notes and QR codes. The tab bar becomes a small indicator: the current section's name, with the others as dots you can still tap. Computers with a mouse keep the tabs.
- **Updates always reach the app.** The page now links its scripts and styles with a fingerprint of their contents. A browser, the app or a caching reverse proxy (such as Nginx Proxy Manager with *Cache Assets* on) can no longer keep showing an old version after the server is updated.
- **QR codes tab works with any server version** (from 1.7.1): it shows your codes in the page instead of switching to Chat.

## Upgrading

- **Server (Unraid)**: update the Flowchat container. If Unraid says no update is available, use *force update* in Advanced View. The page's Settings should then show *Server version 1.8.1*.
- **Android**: the app itself is unchanged from 1.7.1, so installing `Flowchat-1.8.1.apk` is optional.
- **Behind Nginx Proxy Manager**: check that the Flowchat proxy host forwards to the right IP and port, and turn off *Cache Assets* for it.
